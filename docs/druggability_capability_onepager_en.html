Methodological Boundaries & Audit Rules

  • Positive Hypothesis Generator Only: Computational models prioritize candidates and design surgical mutants; formal Tier-1/GO milestones require orthogonal wet-lab validation (co-IP, NanoBRET, SPR, HTRF).
  • AI Confidence ≠ Functional Potency: Boltz-2/AF2 ipTM measures fold plausibility, not binding affinity (homolog negative controls can score >0.90); RF2-PPI has high recall on >1,500 Å² interfaces (43.2%) but low recall on <800 Å² transient SLiMs (4.5%).
  • Dynamics Rescue Static Artifacts: Top docked or mutated poses are gated by explicit-solvent MD (vetoing drifting poses) and double-precision alchemical TI/FEP (eliminating mixed-precision zero-window and static cavity artifacts).
  • Exposure & Cell-Type Framing: Literature activities are gated by clinical free Cmax/Ki occupancy and human single-cell expression; every claim is tagged [FACT], [OBS], [MECH], or [HYP].
