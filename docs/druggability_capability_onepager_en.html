Scientific Boundaries & Rigorous Veracity Standards Audit principles enforced in every external delivery

Structure & Contact Mechanics Boundaries

  • Distinguish observation, mechanics, and hypothesis: PRODIGY physical contacts and explicit-solvent MD trajectories represent dynamic mechanics; docking scores alone do not equate to binding affinity.
  • Single-pose pitfalls avoided: We enforce multi-frame sampling or GPU explicit solvent relaxation before calculating ΔΔG to avoid single-pose minimization artifacts.
  • Never rely on blind AI-folding scores: AlphaFold/Boltz iptm serves as an initial spatial sanity check, never a surrogate for binding free energy. All molecular glue models require experimental crystal anchors (e.g., PDB: 7RUQ).

Deliverable Standards for Stakeholders

  • 100% Pure English Deliverables: All external presentations, one-pagers, and interactive HTML artifacts are authored in English with zero Chinese characters.
  • Zero-Install CIFS Handover: Reports are authored as self-contained files (Base64 embedded figures, CDN-resilient 3D WebGL viewers) accessible via Windows UNC paths (R:\DT\TDE_TV\shared_folder\QYJI\...).
  • Companion Plain-Text Emails: Every deliverable is paired with an unformatted .txt file to enable seamless, copy-paste email handover to biologists without Outlook formatting breakage.
Decision-Grade Evidence Principle: Computational predictions are hypothesis-generating accelerators designed to narrow down vast chemical and sequence spaces. While our physical models achieve high correlation with clinical phenotypes (e.g., predicting the exact loss-of-function effect of UK Biobank variants), formal project milestones require orthogonal biophysical verification (e.g. TR-FRET, SPR, and cell-based phosphorylation assays).
