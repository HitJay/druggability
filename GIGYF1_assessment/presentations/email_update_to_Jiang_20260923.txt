Below is a summary of the two key items regarding the variant annotations.

1. Clarification of the GIGYF1 Pocket Floor Probe and Removal of rs372863955
The variant identifier rs372863955 previously shown on the slide was an erroneous annotation from an automated mapping lookup. As you noted, rs372863955 is located on chromosome 3 (3:136263255) within an intron of PCCB, having no biological connection to GIGYF1 on chromosome 7 (7:100679505-100694481). The perturbation evaluated in our structural model at residue 498 (p.Trp498Cys) was designed as an in silico mutagenesis probe to evaluate the loss of the native tryptophan indole ring at the GYF pocket floor, which imposes a +2.74 kcal/mol penalty and collapses baseline affinity by over 80-fold (Kd shifting from 86.4 uM to 3.85 mM). Because the UK Biobank human genetics evidence for GIGYF1 (Zhao et al., Nat Commun 2021) is driven by a gene-level burden of 27 ultra-rare loss-of-function variants (OR = 6.10, P = 1.8e-12) rather than an individual common missense SNP, this specific residue probe does not possess an independent GWAS rsID. We have removed all phantom rs tags and clarified that p.Trp498Cys serves as a structural disruption probe that mechanistically phenocopies the clinical loss-of-function outcome.

2. Full Verification of Remaining Axis Variants (GRB10 and GRB14)
We re-verified all remaining rs identifiers cited across the axis against Ensembl and dbSNP to ensure complete genomic fidelity. The following markers are confirmed genuine and correctly positioned: (a) GRB14 natural missense coding variant p.Phe90Ile (rs61748245, chr2:164619743, NM_004490.2:c.268T>A), which directly alters aromatic packing on the GIGYF1 GYF domain; (b) GRB14 GWAS lead variant rs13389219 (chr2:164672366), showing genome-wide significance for body fat distribution and insulin resistance; (c) GRB10 imprinted locus regulatory variants rs933360 and rs11555134 (chr7:50690548 and 7:50591496), demonstrating genome-wide association with glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (GSIS) and T2D; and (d) GIGYF1 cis-eQTL rs221781 (chr7:100698285), where reduced transcript expression correlates with elevated fasting glucose (P = 4.8e-15).
