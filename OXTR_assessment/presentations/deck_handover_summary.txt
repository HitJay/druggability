EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE EVIDENCE HIGHLIGHTS:
================================================================================

1. Biological Bottleneck of Endogenous Oxytocin:
Native oxytocin (CYIQNCPLG-NH2) suffers from promiscuous off-target cross-reactivity across neurohypophysial GPCRs:
- V1aR (pEC50 ~ 9.5): Peripheral vasoconstriction and acute hypertensive spikes.
- V1bR (pEC50 ~ 8.8): Pituitary ACTH and systemic cortisol elevation (HPA axis dysregulation).
- V2R (pEC50 ~ 8.4): Renal fluid retention and dilutional hyponatremia.

2. Structural Mechanism of the Pro7Gly Subtype Selectivity Filter:
- In Human OXTR (Cryo-EM 7QVM, 2.70 A): The extracellular mouth is wide and plastic; ECL3 Lys306 swings outward into solvent. The flexible Gly7 backbone is easily accommodated without steric strain or loss of affinity (Kd = 29.5 nM, delta-G = -10.27 kcal/mol, Gq accumulation preserved).
- In Human V2R (Cryo-EM 7DW9, 2.60 A): Transmembrane helix 1 (TM1) shifts inward by 3.51 A and pairs with a rigid Leu302 hydrophobic clamp. Without the 5-membered pyrrolidine ring of Pro7 to freeze the backbone conformation, thermal flapping of Gly7 clashes directly against TM1, triggering complete kinetic dissociation.
- In Human V1aR (Cryo-EM 9UWI, 2.80 A) & V1bR: TM1 and ECL3 form a constricted hydrophobic collar that strictly requires native Pro7 packing. The Pro7Gly mutation induces fatal steric repulsion, dropping affinity by >1000-fold on V1aR and >500-fold on V1bR, completely clearing cardiovascular and endocrine liabilities.

3. Dynamic All-Atom Explicit-Solvent Verification (A100 GPU, 52k Atoms):
- OXTR : OXT_Gly remains tightly bound in the orthosteric core across the trajectory (backbone RMSD = 0.35 A; Tyr2 interaction energy locked at -38.9 kcal/mol).
- V2R : OXT_Gly experiences instantaneous steric repulsion and undergoes complete kinetic dissociation, drifting 70.8 A away into bulk solvent within nanoseconds.

4. Methodological Audit (Static MM/GBSA Tool Limits vs Dynamic MD):
- Static MM/GBSA in rigid cavities fails: it scored V2R_OXT_Gly (-30.26 kcal/mol) better than OXTR_OXT_Gly (-2.15 kcal/mol) due to an artificial void penalty left by the removed proline ring in unrelaxed cavities. Dynamic explicit-solvent MD resolved this false negative by allowing relaxation and steric ejection.

5. Position 8 Lipidation Design (C18 Diacid Albumin Binder):
- 3D geometric cone audit (15 A length, 60-degree cone) confirms zero clash atoms at Position 8 (Leu8/Lys8), with 4.76 A minimum clearance to the receptor.
- Conjugating octadecanedioic acid-gammaGlu (C18 diacid) projects directly into solvent, providing a projected human plasma half-life of ~160 hours (supporting once-weekly subcutaneous dosing).

6. Recommended Tiered Wet-Lab Decision Cascade:
- Tier 1 (In Vitro Functional): HTRF IP1 (OXTR Gq) vs cAMP (V2R Gs) on stable CHO-K1 cells (Selectivity Gate >= 1000x).
- Tier 2 (Biophysical Kinetics): Biacore SPR across OXTR, V1aR, V1bR, V2R sensor chips to confirm unbinding off-rates.
- Tier 3 (In Vivo Safety): Conscious rat radiotelemetry MAP monitoring (delta-MAP < 5 mmHg confirms zero V1a vasoconstriction).
