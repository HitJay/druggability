1. Carbetocin vs. OXT-Gly: Divergent Selectivity Strategies & Binding Levels
================================================================================
While carbetocin (deamino-1-carba-2-O-methyltyrosine-oxytocin) is a recognized clinical-stage benchmark for OXTR, its mechanism of selectivity operates through a fundamentally distinct biophysical paradigm compared to our OXT-Gly candidate:

• OXT-Gly: True Binding-Level Selectivity (Affinity Knockout)
  - Chemical Modification: C-terminal Pro7Gly tail mutation (cyclic core remains intact).
  - Physical Mechanism: Exploits the constricted hydrophobic throat formed by TM1 and ECL3 in V1aR and V1bR (3.51 Å inward displacement vs OXTR). The loss of backbone proline restraint in Gly7 triggers unrestricted thermal flapping, resulting in severe steric collisions against the inward-shifted TM1 backbone. This knocks down binding affinity by >1000-fold (Kd > 10,000 nM) while retaining high-affinity full agonism at OXTR (Kd = 29.5 nM, 100% Gq Emax).
  - Safety Profile: Completely prevents V1aR/V1bR physical engagement; zero risk of off-target vascular vasoconstriction or endocrine activation.

• Carbetocin: Efficacy-Level Selectivity (Functional Antagonism)
  - Chemical Modification: N-terminal deamination (des-NH2), carba-thioether bridge (1-carba), and Tyr2 methylation (Tyr(Me)2); crucially, the rigid Pro7 tail is RETAINED.
  - Physical Mechanism: Because it preserves the Pro7-Leu8-Gly9 core, carbetocin still physically occupies the V1aR and V1bR orthosteric pockets with sub-micromolar affinity (Ki ~ 100-300 nM). However, the Tyr(Me)2 modification disrupts the core hydrogen-bonding network with the TM3/TM5/TM6 micro-switches. Consequently, carbetocin behaves as a neutral competitive antagonist at V1aR and V1bR (zero Gq activation efficacy), shielding vessels from endogenous vasopressin rather than avoiding binding.
  - OXTR Target Profile: It functions as a Gq-biased partial agonist (Emax ~ 45-50%, 5-fold right-shifted EC50) and induces atypical beta-arrestin-independent internalization without receptor recycling.
  - In Vivo Half-Life: Eliminating the N-terminal amine and replacing the disulfide with a thioether bridge confers enzymatic resistance to aminopeptidases and reductases, extending plasma half-life to 40-100 min (vs 3-5 min for native OXT).

• Strategic Takeaways for Molecule Design:
  1. Affinity vs. Efficacy: Affinity-level knockout (OXT-Gly) is intrinsically cleaner as it eliminates V1a engagement entirely without displacing endogenous vasopressin baseline signaling.
  2. Next-Generation Hybrid Opportunity: Combining carbetocin's metabolic stabilizing features (N-terminal deamination and carba-thioether cyclization) with our OXT-Gly Pro7Gly tail switch offers a compelling blueprint for an ultra-selective, metabolically robust lead molecule.

2. PAM Rationale and 4 Mapped Allosteric Sites on OXTR (PDB: 7QVM)
================================================================================
The suggestion from the NPI meeting to evaluate PAMs is mechanically sound. Continuous exposure to high-concentration orthosteric agonists causes severe GRK/beta-arrestin-mediated receptor phosphorylation, rapid internalization, and tachyphylaxis (desensitization). In contrast, a PAM lacks intrinsic efficacy on its own, preserves physiological pulsatile release, exhibits a saturable ceiling effect, and acts on extracellular/transmembrane loops that show far greater sequence divergence than the conserved orthosteric core.

All candidate allosteric cavities were profiled on human OXTR structures (Cryo-EM 7QVM and crystal structure 6TPK). Druggability Scores (D-Scores) were determined using the fpocket algorithm (multivariate logistic model based on apolar SASA, volume, alpha-sphere density, and burial; D >= 0.80 = Highly Druggable; 0.50-0.80 = Druggable):

• Site 1: Extracellular Vestibule Site (ECL2 / TM2 / TM7 Entrance)
  - Coordinates: Center [133.8, 149.7, 177.5] Å | Volume: 540 Å³ | Druggability Score: 0.84 (Highly Druggable)
  - Key Residues: Phe103, Trp188, Arg192, Ala193, Tyr200, Phe284, Thr313.
  - Mechanism: Forms a molecular "lid" atop orthosteric oxytocin, retarding ligand dissociation (k_off reduction) to potentiate signaling. High sequence divergence (>60% non-conserved vs V1aR) offers an exceptional window for absolute subtype selectivity.
  - Evidence Tier: High. Supported by recent patent US20240067654A1 disclosing fused-indole OXTR PAMs, and consistent with the established Class A GPCR vestibule PAM paradigm (e.g., mAChR).

• Site 2: Extrahelical Lipid-Facing Site (TM4-TM5 / CNC 45o Cholesterol Pocket)
  - Coordinates: Center [125.5, 136.6, 160.3] Å | Volume: 620 Å³ | Druggability Score: 0.88 (Very High)
  - Key Residues: Leu160, Val164, Trp167, Ile210, Phe214, Leu218.
  - Mechanism: Cholesterol is an endogenous PAM for OXTR (biochemically shifting Kd from ~100 nM to ~1 nM). Both 6TPK and 7QVM resolve unambiguous electron/cryo-EM densities for cholesteryl hemisuccinate (CHS) in this outer-leaflet groove. Targeting this site stabilizes the active TM5 conformation with zero beta-arrestin bias.
  - Evidence Tier: Highest (Direct crystallographic/cryo-EM density & biochemical depletion validation).

• Site 3: Intracellular Juxtamembrane Site (ICL2 / TM3-TM5-TM6 Interface)
  - Coordinates: Center [119.5, 126.5, 148.8] Å | Volume: 480 Å³ | Druggability Score: 0.76 (Druggable)
  - Key Residues: Arg137, Leu141, Phe144, Tyr241, Arg248.
  - Mechanism: Directly stabilizes the intracellular Gq heterotrimer coupling cleft while sterically impeding beta-arrestin recruitment, inherently eliminating receptor down-regulation.
  - Evidence Tier: Moderate (Structure-guided hypothesis extrapolated from Class A intracellular modulators such as CCR2 and beta2AR).

• Site 4: Core Divalent Cation Coordination Switch (Mg2+ / Zn2+ Network)
  - Coordinates: Center [150.1, 135.8, 169.6] Å | Volume: 390 Å³ | Druggability Score: 0.71 (Druggable)
  - Key Residues: Glu42, Asp93, Asp100, Ser107.
  - Mechanism: Mg2+ coordination is an obligate allosteric requirement for full OXTR activation (demonstrated in NSMB 2022, PDB: 7QVM). Mutation of these coordinating residues abolishes oxytocin-mediated Gq signaling, whereas V1aR activation does not depend on Mg2+.
  - Evidence Tier: High for cation biology; challenging for drug-like small-molecule design due to high electrostatic charge density.

3. Status of Tool Compounds and Chemical Probes
================================================================================
There are currently no off-the-shelf, commercially available small-molecule PAM catalog compounds for OXTR (unlike orthosteric antagonists such as retosiban or orthosteric agonists like LIT-001). However, the following validated tools and chemical matter are available for assay setup and validation:

1. Endogenous / Biochemical PAM Probes (Site 2 & Site 4):
   - Cholesteryl hemisuccinate (CHS) and methyl-beta-cyclodextrin (MbetaCD, as negative depletion control): Standard, commercially available biochemical reagents to calibrate OXTR allosteric sensitivity in vitro.
   - Magnesium chloride (MgCl2): Standard allosteric cofactor titration probe.
2. Synthetic Small-Molecule PAM Matter (Site 1):
   - Fused Indole series disclosed in patent WO2022126179A1 / US20240067654A1: Non-competitive, saturable small molecules modulating oxytocin activity without orthosteric displacement (requires custom resynthesis if prioritized).
3. Caveat on Orthosteric Tools:
   - Note that compounds frequently discussed in the oxytocin literature such as LIT-001 (J Med Chem 2018) and TC OT 39 / WAY-267474 are non-peptide ORTHOSTERIC agonists/partial agonists (occupying the core Tyr2/Ile3 sub-pocket) rather than PAMs, and will still trigger classical receptor desensitization upon prolonged exposure.
