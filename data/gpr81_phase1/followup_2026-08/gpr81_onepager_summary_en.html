Key findings

  • FACTc30 most potent (5 nM) but only 7.4x GPR109A-selective — must be resolved before advancing
  • FACTc5 (0.74 nM) is a partial agonist (Emax 47%) — tool compound, not a lead
  • OBSNeither computational layer ranks potency: Vina score–EC50 r = −0.114; Boltz-2 affinity prob–EC50 r = −0.044 (both ≈ 0)

  • OBSSAR: pyridone ≫ pyrimidinone (N3–Glu153 clash, 47x potency cliff, 8/8 seeds)
