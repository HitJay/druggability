## Finding 1 — 8Z87 large-molecule systematic positive scores [OBS + MECH]

Data: multiseed AZ1/8Z87 = 8/8 seeds +9.98..+10.4 (single cluster). Matched pairs on 8Z87: c22 +7.5, c30 +2.1, c31 +12.7, c35 +1.5, c36 +2.6, c37 +5.0, c38 +6.0 — all positive; same compounds on 8Z8A all negative (-2.8..-7.5). Small acids (CHBA, 3,5-DHBA, 3-OBA) score normally negative on 8Z87.

Interpretation:
- [FACT] AZ1/c30/c3 are sub-50 nM human HCAR1 agonists; a systematic "non-binder" verdict is biologically implausible.
- [MECH] Positive Vina score = steric/electrostatic conflict with the CHBA sub-pocket; 8Z87 is a single rigid conformation and cannot accommodate the Davidsson acyl-urea/pyridone series geometry.
- [OBS] Size alone is not the explanation: c22 (MW ~508, +7.5) vs c21 (MW ~524, -3.2) differ by 10 units at similar MW — specific group/shape incompatibility.
- [HYP] Unverified: protonation/charge state of N-methylpiperazine groups interacting with pocket electrostatics may contribute; would need a charge-state sweep to confirm.

Action: treat 8Z87 as a counter-screen (CHBA sub-pocket compatibility), never as a binder/non-binder verdict for large molecules. Re-dock large molecules with a larger box / flexible side chains, or explicitly label clash-incompatible.

## Finding 2 — 9KT9 receptor preparation failure [OBS + HYP]

- [OBS] 3,5-DHBA→9KT9 redock centroid 5.71 A (vs 0.88 A for CHBA→8Z87, 2.89 A lactate→8Z8A).
- [OBS] Every compound's 9KT9 pose has core contact fraction 0.000; contacts cluster at N-terminal residues (CYS6, CYS7, ARG79, ILE9) — poses sit outside the orthosteric pocket.
- [OBS] After Cα alignment (9KT9 vs 8Z87 backbone RMSD 1.018 A, 281 common residues), 9KT9 poses are 9-17 A away from the 8Z87-frame poses.
- [HYP] Root causes to test: (a) ligand 34D coordinates not in the same frame as receptor chain R (chain/index mismatch in the PDB); (b) box center offset from the true pocket; (c) wrong chain extracted as receptor; (d) receptor prep dropped/kept residues that block the pocket.

Action (P0): verify 34D vs chain R frame, box center, chain identity; re-prepare; redock 3,5-DHBA to < 2 A centroid before trusting any 9KT9 result.

## Finding 3 — matched_pair_summary.json readability [OBS]

7 of 9 compounds positive on 8Z87 mixed with negatives; no per-receptor validity flag. A naive reader will conclude "compounds don't bind". Fix: add per-receptor validity/warning column or split conclusions by receptor.

## Finding 4 — contact stats on positive-score poses [OBS]

AZ1/8Z87 shows "core contact fraction = 0.833" on a +10.1 kcal/mol pose. Clash-distance contacts are not binding evidence. Contact/union/intersection stats on positive or weak poses must be labeled invalid or computed only for clash-free poses.

## Finding 5 — single-layer scoring [OBS]

All conclusions rest on one Vina score per pose. Per project convention (no single docking score as conclusion), add a second layer (MM/GBSA via OpenMM GBn2, or pose-cluster consensus + conserved-contact analysis) for the top-ranked matched pairs on the valid receptors.
