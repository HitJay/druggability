Emapunil's computed off-target profile is narrow, and the PK11195 competition is most plausibly TSPO itself.
Of 21 binder-list targets, 17 had a tractable ligand-bound structure. Emapunil docks into the TSPO anchor pocket 1.09 Å from the crystal PK11195 pose and stays there through 250 ps unrestrained-after-minimisation MD (contacts SER20 / TRP49 / PHE88 — corrected*, see note below), the canonical TSPO pocket). Three candidates show comparable, MD-stable in-pocket poses: GABA-A BZD site (α1/γ2 interface, 6X3X), OPRM1 orthosteric (4DKL), and CAR LBD (1XVP). Everything else is a >3 Å outside-pocket pose, a repelled dock (+3.54 kcal/mol at GRM2), or structurally untestable.
What this is and is not
All scores are supporting computational evidence, not proof of binding. Tiers never claim measured affinity: only TSPO has a measured emapunil interaction (published displacement of [³H]PK11195). Docking scores do not resolve potency or rank across different pockets; the MD layer tests pose stability and pocket geometry, not binding free energy.

2. Methods chain (per target)

  1. Identity resolution — emapunil resolved to CID 6433109 / CHEMBL513922 (XBD-173, AC-5216 are the same record); InChIKey NBMBIEOUVBHEBM-UHFFFAOYSA-N.
  2. Pocket map — for each of the 21 binder-list targets, PDBe mappings API (UniProt → PDB) found ligand-bound structures; pockets with a drug-like co-crystal ligand (≥20 heavy atoms) carried forward.
  3. Validation gate — each pocket first reproduces its own co-crystal ligand: redock (RMSD-recovery) or, where ligand PDBQT construction was unreliable, crystal-pose score-only. Pockets failing the gate are excluded from interpretation.
  4. Emapunil docking — AutoDock Vina, 24 Å box on the pocket, flexible ligand, 9–20 poses.
  5. MD stability + interaction-energy decomposition (FEP-selected pockets) — amber14 ff14SB + OpenFF 2.1.0 (NAGL am1bcc charges), TIP3P-less implicit-free minimisation + 250 ps Langevin MD (300 K, 2 fs, CUDA double), all protein heavy atoms restrained at 5 kcal/mol/Å²; ligand–residue Coulomb+vdW decomposition from the relaxed complex. Raw pair energies overstate charged contacts (no desolvation) — used for ranking only.

6. Answer to the request

"Pick top candidates and evaluate whether their pockets make sense for emapunil." The pre-registered selection (anchor + top-3 + negative control) was carried through MD. The pockets make sense in this order:

  1. TSPO (4RYI) — makes full sense; reproduces the PK11195 competition geometry (1.09 Å) and is MD-stable. Primary explanation for the observed mitochondrial competition.
  2. GABA-A BZD site (6X3X) — makes sense; the α1/γ2 interface pocket accepts emapunil with an MD-stable pose and LEU223/PRO224 hydrophobic contacts, consistent with the alpidem round's conclusion that this family is the second neighbourhood of TSPO-ligand-like chemotypes.
  3. OPRM1 (4DKL) — plausible but flagged: the pose is stable, yet emapunil is neutral and the strongest contact is an acidic anchor position; worth an in vitro binding check before believing it.
  4. CAR (1XVP) — geometrically plausible, previously demoted by the cross-pocket score argument; treat as a watch-list item only.

Recommended next step (experimental): a single digitised assay — [³H]emapunil or [³H]PK11195 displacement on membranes from cells expressing each candidate individually — would collapse this list to the true off-target set. The computational layer has done its job: nothing here justifies excluding TSPO as the competition site, and nothing here proves a second target.

7. Environment and reproducibility notes

  • openfe-md env (openfe 1.12.0, openmm 8.4, openmmtools 0.26.0, openff-toolkit 0.18.0, openff-nagl 0.5.5); Vina docking used the project venv (/home/QYJI/das/druggability/.venv).
  • NAGL am1bcc charges require an explicit model path in this env (openff-gnn-am1bcc-1.0.0.pt) — the method-string lookup raises BadFileSuffixError (openff 0.18 / nagl 0.5.5 mismatch).
  • Hydrogens must be added to the protein before the ligand is added to the Modeller, else amber template-matching rejects the UNL residue.
  • implicit/obc2.xml raises "Unknown variable: k" in openmm 8.4; amber14/tip3p.xml used instead.
  • 6X3X (34k atoms) needs all-heavy-atom positional restraints; CA-only restraints let chain-break regions drift and destabilise the MD.
  • The 4RYI "in-pocket distance" recorded in the f2 JSON was computed against a buggy multi-copy PKA centroid; the corrected value (1.09 Å vs crystal PKA) is written back into the JSON and used here.
  • Unit tests: tests/test_emapunil_dataset.py — 11/11 pass (tier logic, no inflation to "computed-positive", negative-control behaviour, anchor-distance correction).

[*] Correction to interaction-energy contacts (2026-10-19)
The contact values marked "corrected*" supersede the numbers originally published in this report. During a later audit, two computational-setup defects were found in the original contact calculation: the ligand's partial charges were silently overwritten (emapunil, formally neutral, entered the calculation with net charge −2.1 e), and one script variant mixed Coulomb unit conventions. The corrected values above were computed with verified charge assignments (ligand net charge 0 asserted) and consistent units (kJ/mol). Residue identities changed for some pockets (e.g. the originally listed TSPO-site contacts PHE23/ASN151/MET47 are corrected to SER20/TRP49/PHE88). Docking scores, pose-stability MD verdicts and tier assignments in this report are unaffected. Full corrected tables plus a new free-energy layer (computational alanine scanning, 8-window TI anchor-usage ΔΔG) are in the companion supplement: fep_a_alanine_scanning_report.html (same folder).
