FEP-grade computational alanine scanning — do the docked poses actually use the pocket anchors?

Supplement to the emapunil (XBD-173) and FGIN-1-27 ligand-target search reports · 2026-10-19 · all energies kJ/mol unless stated

Erratum on the earlier interaction-energy contacts. The "top contacts" tables in the two delivered reports were computed on a system in which the ligand net charge was silently corrupted (−2.1 e for formally neutral emapunil) by a forcefield-setup side effect, and one script variant mixed Coulomb unit conventions. The affected residue identities and magnitudes are therefore not reliable — e.g. the reported TSPO-site contacts PHE23 −19.6 / ASN151 −10.0 / MET47 −7.8 for emapunil are artifacts (PHE23 is ≈ 0 in the corrected calculation). The corrected contacts and the new free-energy layer below supersede those tables. Docking scores, MD pose-stability verdicts and tier assignments in the original reports are unaffected.

Method (one paragraph)

For each pocket we took the MD-relaxed complex, rebuilt the force field cleanly (amber14 ff14SB protein + OpenFF 2.1.0 ligand with NAGL AM1BCC charges, ligand net charge asserted 0), restrained protein heavy atoms (5 kcal/mol/Å²) and ligand heavy atoms (1 kcal/mol/Å²) to the relaxed pose, minimised, then annihilated the non-bonded interaction between the ligand and one anchor residue's side chain (charges and well-depths scaled by 1−λ) over 8 λ windows with 27.5 ps sampling each. The integrated cost, ΔΔGannihilate, is the free-energy price of removing that specific contact: positive = the ligand uses this residue. This is the standard computational-alanine-scanning formulation (Massova & Kollman 1999) — the same question as a residue→Ala mutation FEP, implemented without topology surgery.

Negative-control reading

The 4XAQ (GRM2) poses are surface-docked, not in a pocket — that is why they were used as controls. The nonzero control ΔΔG values (+9.1 emapunil, +23.6 FGIN) mean the ligand genuinely interacts with whatever surface it was docked onto; they do not indicate specific binding. The control's message stays: the GRM2 docking pose is not pocket-like (repelled in docking, 10–11 Å outside the pocket), so GRM2 remains a non-candidate. FGIN's larger control value is consistent with its higher lipophilicity (more non-specific surface adhesion).

Scope & caveats. Sampling is short (27.5 ps per window): the ΔΔG values are directional estimates for ranking anchor usage, not converged free energies, and the vacuum protocol carries no solvent/entropy terms — absolute numbers must not be read as binding energies. The annihilation measures the contact at the restrained docked/relaxed geometry. Per-pocket comparisons are meaningful; cross-pocket magnitude comparisons are not (different pockets, different poses). As throughout the campaign: computational scores are supporting evidence, not proof of binding — only measured data (radioligand displacement etc.) can establish binding.
