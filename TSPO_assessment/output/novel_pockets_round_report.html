Novel-pocket screening on the PK11195 binder-list proteins, with emapunil docking + MD in the new pockets

TSPO ligand target search, request from Zheng Cao, 2026-10-19 · Round ws6 · follow-up to the 2026-10-19 PK11195 binder list (21 distinct proteins)

Verdict. fpocket screening of all 17 usable experimental structures behind the binder list finds 29 pocket candidates that are disjoint from every literature/co-crystal-reported pocket. Emapunil docks into 15 of them at scan tier; 10 survive 3-seed confirmation; 7 of the 10 MD-run pockets hold their pose through 250 ps restrained MD. The strongest novel site is on TSPO itself: a second, druggability-score-0.99 cavity (4RYI pocket 26, ~1300 A^3, zero residue overlap with the PK11195 pocket, interface of the dimeric crystal) that binds emapunil at −7.6 kcal/mol and stays within 1.4 A through MD. All computational evidence; no free-energy claims are made.

1. What was screened

Every protein on the 29-row PK11195 binder list with at least one experimental structure (verified against UniProt/RCSB cross-references, not memory): 14 structures already carrying controls from the emapunil/FGIN campaigns, plus new structural coverage found this round — GRM5 (human 7TM, 4OO9), TMEM97/sigma-2 (bovine; human has no structure; 7M93/PB28 and 9ZIR/zervimesine), and re-checks confirming NPSR, PMP22, ALOX15-human have no experimental structure at all (screening impossible; recorded as gaps, not skipped), and TSPO-human P30536 still has no PDB entry (BcTSPO 4RYI remains the anchor model).

fpocket 4.0 recovered the known (co-crystal) pocket in 17/17 structures — the method control that makes "no pocket found / new pocket found" statements meaningful on this receptor set.

2. How "novel" was decided

exclusionA pocket is only called novel when (a) its residues do not recover the co-crystal (literature-reported) pocket of its own structure (<30% residue overlap, centroid >3 A from every copy of the pocket-defining ligand — this also kills the "same site on the other protomer" false positives); (b) druggability score ≥0.20; (c) volume ≥250 A^3; (d) ≥20 alpha spheres on ≥12 residues. Pockets within 3 A of a bound lipid (OLA/OLC/MPG...) are tagged lipid-adjacent rather than excluded — they may be the lipid-facing cavities already discussed in the literature, so their novelty claim is weaker. Independent literature inventory: Europe PMC pocket-structure queries per target (18 targets, PMIDs saved to novelq_p3_literature_pockets.json); no candidate pocket matches a reported pocket in that inventory.

5. Reading the headline pockets

  • 4RYI pocket 26 (TSPO) computed-md — the most decision-relevant finding for the TSPO project. DS 0.985 / 1301 A^3, formed at the dimer interface of the BcTSPO crystal (residues from both chains: TM1/TM2/TM3 on one face, helix-helix groove on the other), zero overlap with the PK11195 pocket. Emapunil: -7.64 scan, -7.60/-7.64/-7.65 hit tier, MD drift 1.44 A, dominant contact MET1-region hydrophobics. Caveat (MECH, not FACT): the site only exists as detected in the dimeric crystal packing; whether the equivalent cleft is accessible in monomeric membrane TSPO is exactly what a longer all-atom membrane MD would test — recommended as the follow-up before any wet-lab probe design.
  • TMEM97 (sigma-2) pockets computed-md — 7M93 pocket 18 (MD-stable, drift 2.09 A, top contact ARG155, -126 kcal-equiv) is the clean TMEM97 result; the corresponding site on 9ZIR (pocket 11, same TM2/TM5-TM6 region, docking confirmed -7.56..-7.58) came out MD-unstable (drift 5.7 A) — the dynamics stage rejected it, so 7M93_p18 alone carries the claim. Neither is the PB28/zervimesine orthosteric pocket; the region recurs across two independent crystal forms, which is what makes it worth reporting.
  • 7F61 pocket 15 (HRH3) computed-md — intracellular-side cavity, best raw score of the round (-9.23), MD drift 3.98 A (stable but at the threshold; treat as borderline). 4WB9 pocket 1 (ALDH1A1) — docks at -8.76 but the MD interaction energy is net positive (+455.8, LYS105 electrostatics dominating): the pose survives but the contact pattern is electrostatically unfavourable; report as docking-only evidence, not a binder candidate.
  • GABA-A 6X3X pockets 78/128 — both MD-stable (drift 1.75/1.77 A); p128 is the a/g-interface TMD cavity adjacent to the known neurosteroid site literature (lipid-adjacent flag). 4DJH p53 (OPRK1) — -9.09 docking, MD-stable (2.30 A, top ALA417); worth noting because OPRK1 was the canonical score-vs-MD disagreement case in the anchor round — this pocket passes both gates. 4DKL p2 / 6HUG p114 — docking engaged but MD-unstable (drift 5.9/5.2 A): the dynamics stage rejected what the score liked, exactly as in the OPRK1 precedent.

6. Scope and limitations (read before citing)

  • No FEP / no binding free energies. Deliverable is docking (ranked, seed-confirmed) + 250 ps restrained-MD pose stability + interaction-energy decomposition. IE sums are raw Coulomb+vdW without desolvation — ranking use only.
  • fpocket sees one conformation per structure. Pockets that open only in other states (or in membrane context) are invisible to this screen; "no novel pocket found on X" means "none detectable in the screened structure(s)".
  • Interface pockets (4RYI p26, GABA-A pockets, 4DJH p53) depend on the crystal's oligomer packing.
  • Bovine TMEM97 (94% identity) stands in for the structure-less human sigma-2; A147T-analogous species caveats apply as in the anchor round.
  • Emapunil is a neutral, fairly rigid ligand; its docking success in a cavity does not transfer to bulkier TSPO ligands.
