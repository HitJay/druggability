Answer to the requester: (1) PK11195's plausible target set = TSPO + CAR + PXR + GABA-A + a µM noise band; the T2D-like benefit is most consistent with CAR activation → fasting-like hepatic state, not TSPO. (3) Etifoxine's plausible set = TSPO (measured but occupancy-impossible) + GABA-A (µM) + microbiome/bile-acid axis; the weight-loss phenotype cannot be TSPO-mediated at tested exposures.

After per-pocket z-score normalisation against the official 2.94M-molecule background (100k subsample), decoys outrank true ligands: lisinopril z=1.73 vs PK11195 z=0.83 on TSPO; glycine z=2.13 on CAR; emapunil→TSPO only z=0.10. The plan's cross-pocket comparability concern is confirmed empirically. Contingency applied: DrugCLIP reverse output = Tier 4 exploratory, excluded from all conclusions. Fixing it would require contrastive fine-tuning on pocket-competition pairs — out of scope.

4. Limits

  • All computational scores are supporting evidence, not proof of binding; only measured data reach Tier 1–2.
  • Species: TSPO ligand affinity is rs6971(A147T)-polymorphic — emapunil 12.6× sensitive, PK11195 1.2×; human vs rodent differs. All evidence is species/genotype-annotated in the CSV.
  • PK11195 racemate vs R-enantiomer changes interpretation; both IDs tracked (CHEMBL481537 / CHEMBL416715).
  • desethyl-etifoxine: active-metabolite claim unresolved (0 literature hits in EPMC) — structure and potency unknown.
  • Mouse free-plasma PK for etifoxine is unavailable publicly; the occupancy exclusion is inferred allometrically (HYP pending mouse PK).
  • Sources skipped with rationale: BindingDB REST retired (404), PDSP unreachable, STITCH resolve garbled, SwissTargetPrediction needs a browser session flow.

6. Recommended next steps (for the biology team)

  1. Test CAR-dependence of PK11195's metabolic effect (CAR-null hepatocytes / CAR antagonist) — highest-value single experiment implied by the evidence.
  2. For etifoxine weight loss: mouse PK + free-plasma measurement at 50 mg/kg i.p., and a microbiome-transfer experiment to separate host vs microbial causality.
  3. For emapunil: replicate the T2D phenotype first (with TSPO-KO as the specificity arm) before target-fishing further — public target space is empty.
  4. Consider Ro5-4864 as the TSPO-selective positive control in any such arm.

8.3 DBI/endozepine axis (literature synthesis)

No direct PK11195-as-DBI-antagonist paper exists. Key context: insulin evokes endozepine release from NTS astrocytes acting via the GABA-A benzodiazepine site on hepatic glucose production (PMID:40976282); TSPO–allopregnanolone–GABA-A appetite axis reviewed with etifoxine as a tool ligand (PMID:34343461); glial ODN proposed for obesity therapy (PMID:41633838). With DBI highly expressed in liver/adipose/hypothalamus (GTEx), the endogenous axis sits exactly where the phenotypes are. HYP: PK11195/emapunil may perturb DBI signalling — the most plausible indirect route for any emapunil metabolic effect.

8.4 Boltz-2 local co-folding (completed 2026-10-15)

Ran on the local HPC GPU with locally staged weights (no BioLib dependency). Inputs: 4RYI full construct (181 aa) + PK11195 SMILES, and the 1XVP CAR-LBD construct (246 aa) + PK11195 SMILES. Outputs in output/boltz/ (run_smoke, run_car).

TSPO + PK11195. Affinity head P(bind) = 0.60 / 0.76 / 0.92 across three diffusion draws; interface confidence moderate (iptm 0.61–0.64, ipde 2.8–3.4 Å). The pose sits inside the TM bundle and contacts the same TM4/TM5 region as the 4RYI co-crystal pocket (90/94/145 shared), but with only 3/16 reference residues in common. OBS: same-domain but not pocket-precise — the NMR co-crystal remains the site-level evidence (as established); the Boltz pose is exploratory only.

CAR LBD + PK11195. De-novo-folded LBD (sequence-only input, no template): Cα RMSD vs 1XVP chain A = 5.65 Å over the aligned core (correct fold), iptm 0.80, complex_plddt 0.76, and P(bind) = 0.41–0.43. The key result: after structural superposition, the predicted PK11195 centroid is 2.52 Å from the co-crystal agonist F15, and 9/12 of its contacting residues coincide with the experimental ligand pocket, including the H11/H12 activation region (Leu305/326/346/349/436). OBS: an independent structure method (de-novo co-folding, no experimental coordinates used) reproduces the PK11195–CAR pocket assignment — the CAR structural arm now rests on Vina (−10.89 kcal/mol) and de-novo co-folding.

Affinity-head caveat: per the GPR81 calibration, the affinity head has no potency-ranking validity within or across series; the TSPO P(bind) (0.76 median) > CAR P(bind) (0.42) ordering is directionally consistent with TSPO being the primary target and is reported as structural-plausibility evidence only.
