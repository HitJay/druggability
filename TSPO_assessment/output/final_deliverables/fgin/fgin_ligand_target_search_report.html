6. Answer to the request

FGIN-1-27 behaves like emapunil in kind but not in degree. Both ligands reproduce the TSPO anchor pose tightly (FGIN 0.7 Å, emapunil 1.1 Å), and both show a flat band of plausible secondary pockets. The differences worth noting:

  • CAR LBD is FGIN's best-scoring off-target pocket (−9.98) and MD-stable — for both ligands this pocket keeps coming back, but it was already demoted once for the cross-pocket score argument; treat as watch-list.
  • CNR1 gains an MD-stable pose for FGIN (emapunil was in-pocket but never MD-tested). The hydrophobic orthosteric pocket suits the dihexyl tail; SER410 contact is H-bond-assisted (no desolvation counted).
  • OPRK1 looked dockable by score alone (−8.25) but the pre-registered MD stage killed it — exactly what the negative-control methodology is for.
  • Unlike emapunil, no candidate reaches the anchor's score — FGIN's computed profile is even more TSPO-dominated than emapunil's.

Bottom line: FGIN-1-27's PK11195 competition is TSPO, consistent with its measured pharmacology; the computed off-target shortlist (CAR, CNR1, watch-list OPRM1/GABA-A/PXR) mirrors emapunil's and remains within docking resolution of the anchor — one displacement panel would settle all of them at once.
