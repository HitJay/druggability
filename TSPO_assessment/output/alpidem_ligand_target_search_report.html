Answer to the requester: Beyond the five you list (TSPO/PBR, CBR/GABA-A, α1-acid glycoprotein, albumin — all confirmed in the data), the additional plausible targets are PXR (NR1I2) at 0.19–0.48 µM (the one newly detected nM-adjacent off-target) and BSEP/ABCB11 at 4.8–9.2 µM plus a CYP/nuclear-receptor µM noise band. The 70% ChEMBL similarity neighbourhood (11 analogues) contains only TSPO and GABA-A binding families — the drug is a two-target compound at pharmacological concentrations. Zolpidem is the clean differentiation comparator: TSPO IC50 4.7 µM vs GABA-A Ki 14–27 nM, i.e. TSPO-negative, which supports alpidem-specific TSPO attribution.

3. Safety-pharmacology anchor (why alpidem left the market)

DILI chain (each link measured): 4 clinical acute-hepatitis cases incl. one fulminant death post-transplant (PMID:7750686, 7750714, 7875405) ← severe GSH depletion + metabolic activation in rat hepatocytes at 250–500 µM, worse with CYP1A induction; zolpidem clean even at 500 µM (PMID:11602696) ← high covalent binding to human liver microsomal protein (DILI-positive reference set, PMID:19720731) ← BSEP inhibition 4.8–9.2 µM (3 assay families) + mitochondrial membrane-potential disruption at 3.8 µM (Tox21). Zolpidem — same imidazopyridine scaffold, no TSPO binding, no GSH depletion, no DILI — is the controlled contrast. Withdrawn in France 1994, one year after launch.

Similarity closure — clean boundary

ChEMBL 70% similarity around CHEMBL54349 returns 11 analogues (2-phenylindol-3-ylglyoxylamide / imidazopyridine TSPO chemistry). Their complete activity surface: TSPO (n=24 records, best 0.4–0.5 nM), GABA-A (n=12, 0–130 nM), CYP/ADMET band, nothing else ≥3 records. Whatever target family is absent here is genuinely absent from public data.

6. Limits

  • All computational scores are supporting evidence, not proof of binding; only measured data reach Tier 1–2.
  • TSPO potency is rodent-dominated (rat kidney/brain); the human A147T (rs6971) genotype question was not resolvable from public rows — relevant if alpidem is ever re-profiled (emapunil 12.6×, PK11195 1.2× sensitivity as calibration).
  • ChEMBL holds zero activity records for CHEMBL54349 (name search also fails; the record is InChIKey/SMILES-resolved only) — the entire measured surface comes via PubChem-mirrored ChEMBL source assays + literature. This is a data-coverage artefact, not a clean-target claim.
  • Zolpidem comparator fetched 500/637 curated records (EBI API instability); the TSPO-negative conclusion rests on the record present (IC50 4.7 µM) plus literature consistency.
  • Liver/plasma concentration ratio for alpidem is unknown publicly; the PXR and BSEP exposure statements are plasma-anchored and marked uncertain/HYP accordingly.
  • Active-metabolite claims: the three metabolites detected in rat (PMID:1680622) and human (PMID:1687121) have no public structure/potency records — "unresolved", not "inactive".
  • Sources skipped with rationale: BindingDB REST retired, PDSP unreachable (same as the prior run).

7. Suggested next steps (for the biology team)

  1. If TSPO re-profiling is intended: run alpidem against human WT vs A147T TSPO — the literature surface is rat-heavy and the clinical genotype question is open.
  2. If the DILI chain is the interest: BSEP IC50 + GSH depletion are the two measured anchors; hepatocyte-to-plasma concentration data would upgrade the BSEP route from HYP.
  3. For any anxiolytic re-formulation concept: alpidem's differentiation vs zolpidem is TSPO occupancy + partial GABA-A efficacy — a re-design should preserve both, not TSPO alone.
